Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026
Last Updated: August 18, 2026

Contril ("Contril", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Contril AI Chief of Staff mobile application and services. We are dedicated to protecting your executive data, privacy, and system security.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure: Contril's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, profile picture, and authentication tokens provided via Supabase Authentication and Google OAuth.
  • Email & Calendar Data: When you connect Gmail or Google Calendar, Contril accesses unread message headers, senders, subject lines, message snippets, and calendar event schedules.
  • AI Interaction & Prompts: Text prompts and voice commands entered into Contril AI.
  • On-Device Price Comparison Data: When you initiate product price comparisons, Contril inspects product title and pricing nodes within shopping applications via on-device automation.
  • Transaction Data: Subscription tier, transaction IDs, and billing status processed securely via Razorpay. Contril never stores raw credit card or UPI PINs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Executive Briefings: To synthesize morning schedule and priority email summaries.
  • AI Reply Drafting: To prepare contextual draft replies for your review and approval.
  • Inbox Hygiene: To identify unwanted newsletters/promotions and move them to Trash upon your explicit confirmation.
  • Price Intelligence: To compare real-time product prices across e-commerce apps on your device.

3. Third-Party AI & Cloud Services

Contril utilizes trusted third-party service providers to power its features:

  • Google Gemini AI API: Used to analyze email snippets and generate executive briefings and draft responses. Data sent to the Gemini API is processed ephemerally and is not used to train generalized foundation models without consent.
  • Supabase: Provides encrypted database and authentication infrastructure.
  • Razorpay: PCI-DSS certified payment gateway for subscription processing.

4. Accessibility Service Disclosure

Contril uses the Android AccessibilityService API exclusively for user-initiated price comparison automation across e-commerce apps. It does not monitor keystrokes, harvest passwords, or access personal messages from other applications.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Contril enforces strict data retention limits. Activity logs and temporary tokens are automatically purged every 30 days. You may disconnect connected services anytime via the in-app Settings screen or request complete account deletion by contacting privacy@contril.app.
